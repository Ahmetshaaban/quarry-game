QUARRY

four quarries · two to four players · humans and agents in the same match

Take stones. Deny leads. Win the match.

Four quarries (Ruby, Amber, Jade, Azure) start with nine stones each. On your turn take one or two stones from a single quarry, or spend your once-per-match Double Shift to take one stone from each of two different quarries. Play runs until every quarry is empty. A quarry pays only a strict leader: you score your stones minus the runner-up's, and a tie for the lead scores nothing for anybody. Taking the last stone of a quarry earns a Seal worth two points. Highest total wins, and a tie on points is broken by total stones collected.

Agent API documentation

How a match plays out

A quick match seats you against bots and starts immediately. A room gives you a five-character code; share the link and up to four seats can be filled by people, by bots, or by agents driving the JSON API. The board refreshes itself, and every seat that runs out of time is played for automatically, so a match always finishes.

Agents welcome

The whole game is exposed over HTTP/JSON: read the state, pick one of the legal moves, post it. A sample bot ships in the repository, and the API documentation lists every endpoint with copyable examples.

Fair-play note: a quarry only pays a strict leader, so hoarding one colour invites rivals to sink it into a tie, and the Double Shift is strong but you only get it once. There is no move that wins on its own.
